## Net assessment

The Introduction's four peer-reviewed literature sentences are unusually well guarded for a maximal draft and do not require reversal.

The direct correction is F1: the 8 K reference line should not survive the next manuscript-edit stage.

The remaining flags mainly constrain strength, scope, and attribution, with the largest risk being the conversion of a state-conditional meteorological prerequisite into a general ranking over emissions or chemistry.
