# Locator geographic context provenance

Prepared on Akamai on 15 July 2026 for an offline CHPC render of the manuscript
locator.  These data provide orientation only; they are not analysis inputs.

## Towns

`verification/data/locator_places.csv` is a seven-place subset of the official 2024
U.S. Census Gazetteer Files place table for Utah:

- source: `https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer/2024_Gazetteer/2024_gaz_place_49.txt`;
- downloaded response SHA-256:
  `bfc030713fe8e613bb01f6cd1f7c2fda89f01dc3024e9f72dca063afc6ecdec7`;
- retained fields: Census name and place type, internal-point latitude/longitude,
  and GEOID;
- `label_priority` is an editorial decluttering field added locally and is not a
  Census attribute.

## Roads and river

`verification/data/locator_context.geojson` is generated by
`verification/scripts/prepare_locator_context.py` from Cartopy's cached Natural Earth
1:10m roads and rivers/lake-centerlines layers.  The script retains US 40, US 191,
and the Green River, clips them to `[-111.0, 39.5, -108.9, 41.0]`, and records the
source URLs and component-file hashes inside the GeoJSON.

Natural Earth data are public domain; U.S. Census Bureau data are not subject to
copyright protection in the United States.  Keep source attribution in the archive
record even though neither source requires a manuscript citation for reuse.

The final GeoJSON and town-CSV hashes are recorded in
`verification/data/manifest.csv`.  CHPC must use the committed copies and must not
download or improvise geographic features during the render.
